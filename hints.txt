In skip-gram the input is the center word and the predictions are the context words within a certain range before and after the current word.

skip gram:
the word itself -> the context words

Continuous bag-of-words model: predicts the middle word based on surrounding context words. The context consists of a few words before and after the current (middle) word. This architecture is called a bag-of-words model as the order of words in the context is not important

bag of words:
the context words -> the word given



The neural network has softmax output layer that has probabilities of every word in the vocabulary.
